# FORMA: serviços, cursos, grupos e matrículas

## Resulting behavior

Services remain appointments, with their original records, payments and attendance. New course classes are prepared under Matrículas rather than created as service appointments. Legacy classes are still editable in the agenda. A service contact can be reused from its existing appointment or from the CRM, preserving the original appointment and its receipts. Ambiguous phone/name matches require manual review; no background bulk contact import runs.

Matrículas has Cursos y grupos and Alumnos matriculados. Staff creates a course and its module names, then a group with instructor, room, capacity, fee, installments and meetings. A weekday generator prepares an editable list of dates; staff can remove holidays or change times individually. Initial/final markers and the student timetable derive from these explicit meetings. A group occupies the instructor/room once regardless of how many students enroll.

Enrollment, seat, optional advance and optional portal invitation are one database transaction. Failed validation rolls back the whole action. UUID-based retries return the confirmed result without repeating the advance. A unique prior commercial enrollment is linked when completing its course conditions. A prior financial enrollment must be explicitly selected: this retains its amount, payments and identity. Creating a duplicate contract for the same active ungrouped course is rejected. Further partial payments use the existing institutional receipt workflow and update remaining debt and the student's private portal.

Groups and courses have version checks. Group pricing edits apply to future enrollment contracts. Changed meetings update the student's dates and create an in-app notice. Closing a group frees its resources while retaining the former meetings and payments. Cancelling a student enrollment frees a seat; reactivation checks capacity. Cancellation does not silently forgive debt or issue a refund.

The instructor schedule reads explicit group meetings separately from service reservations. Instructors see their own course meetings. Database availability checks operate in both directions for courses/services and on named rooms between services. Old appointments without a room retain an unknown room; no room is guessed for historical records. Instructor conflicts remain enforced.

## Unified financial presentation

The existing summary now combines incoming/outgoing bars with cumulative received-income growth and comparison with the previous same elapsed period. Day/week/month/year controls remain. Category bars, payment-method donut and a server-aggregated services/courses/unassigned breakdown derive from the full receipt ledger, not the capped movement list. Contracts and pending payments are not cash receipts. Net cash flow is not presented as profit. The day chart uses actual recorded dates; it does not invent a receipt hour from the record creation timestamp.

## Verification and limits

Database acceptance tests cover original-data preservation on rerun, group collisions, named service rooms, capacity, partial advances, rollback on failed advance, retries, prior-contract linking, preservation of prior receipts, private student meetings, instructor restrictions, direct-table denial and server-derived financial streams. UI tests cover separate navigation, module editing, weekday generation, contact reuse, an uncertain payment result retried once, and clearing data on sign-out. Existing agenda/CRM/budget/banking pilot/PWA checks are retained.

No automatic transfer, PIX processing, fiscal invoice, push notification, WhatsApp send, bank synchronization, or external email is activated by this change. Existing new-receipt refund and contract renegotiation limitations still apply. Portal notifications are in-app. The fixed service calendar grid remains the existing 13:00–18:00 window; explicit academic meetings outside that window are listed in the course-meeting section and student portal.
